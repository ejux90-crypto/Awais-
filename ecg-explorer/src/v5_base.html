        Systematic Bedside Tachycardia & ECG Algorithm

        Step 1: Unstable vs Stable?

        • Unstable (hypotension, altered mental status, shock, ischemic chest pain, acute pulmonary edema):
        • Regular/Irregular with pulse → Synchronized Cardioversion (sedate if conscious).
        • Pulseless VT or VF → Immediate CPR + Unsynchronized Defibrillation.
        • 2025 AHA first synchronized shock: narrow regular & monomorphic VT 100 J; AF & flutter ≥ 200 J; polymorphic VT → unsynchronized (defibrillation dose). If device unknown: maximum.

        Step 2: Narrow (< 120 ms) vs Wide (≥ 120 ms)

        • Narrow Regular: Sinus Tach, AVNRT, Orthodromic AVRT, Focal AT, Atrial Flutter (2:1).
        • Narrow Irregular: AFib, MAT (≥ 3 P shapes), Atrial Flutter w/ variable block.
        • Wide Regular: VT until proven otherwise! (vs SVT w/ aberrancy or Antidromic AVRT).
        • Wide Irregular: AFib with BBB / aberrancy (most common), Pre-excited AFib (WPW — FBI), Polymorphic VT / Torsades.

        Step 3: P Wave & RP Interval (Narrow Regular)

        • Buried P' (RP < 70 ms, pseudo-S in II / r' in V1): Typical AVNRT.
        • Short RP (> 70 ms, RP < PR): Orthodromic AVRT.
        • Long RP (P' before QRS, RP > PR): Focal AT, Sinus Tach, Atypical AVNRT.
        • Sawtooth ~150 bpm: Atrial Flutter (2:1 block).

        Step 4: Vagal / Adenosine Diagnostic Test

        • Terminates abruptly: AV-node–dependent reentry (AVNRT, Orthodromic AVRT).
        • Pauses QRS & unmasks waves: Atrial-only rhythm (Flutter sawtooth, Focal AT P', AFib).
        • NEVER give AV blockers (adenosine, diltiazem, verapamil, β-blocker) in Pre-excited AFib (FBI)!
        • Adenosine is harmful in unstable irregular or polymorphic WCT, and contraindicated in asthma.
